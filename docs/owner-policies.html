Owner Policies

Best practices for successful property management

Welcome to Flatery! As a property owner, these policies guide you through managing your listings, tenants, and rental operations effectively. Following these guidelines ensures compliance and a positive experience for all parties.

1. Owner Eligibility & Verification

  • Must be the legal owner or authorized agent of the property
  • Valid property ownership documents required for verification
  • Government-issued photo ID mandatory for registration
  • Property must have clear legal title without disputes
  • All information provided must be accurate and up-to-date
  • Tax compliance documentation may be required

2. Property Listing Standards

  • Provide accurate property details, photos, and amenities
  • Photos should be clear, recent, and represent actual property condition
  • Pricing must be competitive and reasonable for the market
  • Update availability status promptly to avoid confusion
  • Disclose all property conditions, restrictions, and issues honestly
  • Remove or mark properties as inactive when no longer available
  • Misleading information may result in listing removal or account suspension

3. Rental Agreements

  • Always execute a written rental agreement before possession
  • Agreement should include rent amount, payment terms, duration, and deposit details
  • Clearly define maintenance responsibilities
  • Include termination clauses and notice period requirements
  • Register the agreement as per local rent control laws (where applicable)
  • Both parties should retain signed copies
  • Use legally compliant agreement templates

4. Tenant Selection & Screening

  • Screen tenants fairly based on legitimate criteria
  • Verify identity, employment, and references
  • Conduct background checks where legally permitted
  • Do not discriminate based on religion, caste, gender, or other protected categories
  • Clearly communicate property rules and expectations upfront
  • Document tenant information and agreements properly
  • Maintain tenant privacy and data confidentiality

5. Rent & Payment Management

  • Set rent at fair market rates
  • Specify payment due dates clearly in the agreement
  • Accept payments through verifiable, traceable methods
  • Issue receipts for all payments received
  • Track payment history through Flatery's platform
  • Notify tenants of rent increases with adequate notice (60-90 days)
  • Handle security deposits as per legal requirements
  • Refund deposits within stipulated time after final inspection

6. Property Maintenance

  • Ensure property is habitable and meets safety standards
  • Respond to maintenance requests within reasonable timeframes
  • Major repairs (plumbing, electrical, structural) are owner's responsibility
  • Conduct periodic property inspections with advance notice
  • Maintain essential amenities and utilities
  • Keep common areas (if applicable) clean and functional
  • Address safety hazards immediately
  • Use Flatery's complaint system for tracking maintenance issues

7. Tenant Rights & Privacy

  • Respect tenant's right to peaceful possession
  • Provide advance notice (typically 24-48 hours) before property visits
  • Do not enter the property without tenant's permission except in emergencies
  • Maintain confidentiality of tenant information
  • Do not harass tenants for rent or other matters
  • Honor the lease term and do not force premature eviction
  • Follow legal eviction procedures if necessary

8. PG Management Guidelines

  • Clearly define and communicate house rules
  • Maintain cleanliness and hygiene in common areas
  • Ensure adequate facilities for all occupants
  • Set and enforce visitor policies
  • Provide proper security measures
  • Maintain separate rent tracking for each occupant
  • Address conflicts between occupants fairly
  • Comply with local PG licensing requirements

9. Legal Compliance

  • Comply with local rent control and tenancy laws
  • Pay applicable property taxes on time
  • Report rental income as per tax regulations
  • Obtain necessary permits/licenses for rental operations
  • Maintain property insurance coverage
  • Follow building codes and safety regulations
  • Provide mandatory safety equipment (fire extinguishers, smoke detectors)
  • Keep records of all transactions and agreements

10. Prohibited Practices

  • Listing properties you don't own without proper authorization
  • Accepting cash payments without providing receipts
  • Discriminating against tenants based on protected characteristics
  • Withholding security deposits without valid reasons
  • Forcing tenants to vacate without legal grounds
  • Entering the property without permission or notice
  • Misrepresenting property condition or amenities
  • Operating unlicensed commercial rentals
  • Violating tenant privacy or safety

11. Communication Best Practices

  • Respond to tenant inquiries promptly
  • Use Flatery's messaging system for documentation
  • Be professional and courteous in all communications
  • Provide clear instructions for rent payment and maintenance requests
  • Document all agreements and changes in writing
  • Keep tenants informed about property-related updates
  • Address complaints and concerns seriously

12. Platform Usage Guidelines

  • Use Flatery's dashboard to manage all property operations
  • Keep property information and availability updated
  • Utilize payment tracking and reporting features
  • Leverage analytics to optimize property performance
  • Do not circumvent the platform to avoid fees (where applicable)
  • Report technical issues or bugs promptly
  • Participate in owner feedback programs to improve the platform
  • Maintain account security and confidentiality

Important Note

These policies provide general guidance for property owners. Specific local laws, regulations, and your rental agreements take precedence. Flatery is a management platform and is not liable for disputes between owners and tenants. Consult legal professionals for specific situations.

Violation of these policies may result in account suspension or termination.
